Before you play

Tournament rules

  1. Register with your name and Pokémon Player ID.
  2. Submit your decklist before registration closes. Changes are locked once registration ends.
  3. Payment is verified manually by the organizer; registration alone does not confirm payment.
  4. Player check-in and match reporting will become available when those features are enabled.
  5. Decklists are not shown publicly on the bracket.
